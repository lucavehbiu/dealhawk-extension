## 💡 PRO TIPS

**Respond FAST**: Contractors who respond within 1 hour are 7x more likely to win the job.

**Follow Up 3 Times**: Most customers don't say yes on the first contact. Follow up at 0 hours, 3 days, and 7 days.

**Send Photos**: Include before/after photos of similar jobs in your quotes.

**Ask for Reviews**: After every completed job, text a Google Review link.
