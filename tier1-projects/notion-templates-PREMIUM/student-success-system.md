### Study Techniques Library

**Pomodoro Technique** (Default for most subjects)
- 25 min focused work
- 5 min break
- After 4 pomodoros: 15-30 min break
- Perfect for: Problem sets, writing, reading

**Active Recall** (Best for memorization)
- Study material
- Close books
- Write down everything you remember
- Check what you missed
- Repeat
- Perfect for: Sciences, vocabulary, facts

**Spaced Repetition** (Long-term retention)
- Review after 1 day
- Review after 3 days
- Review after 7 days
- Review after 14 days
- Perfect for: Exams, cumulative finals

**Feynman Technique** (Deep understanding)
- Pick a concept
- Explain it like you're teaching a 10-year-old
- Identify gaps in explanation
- Review and simplify
- Perfect for: Complex concepts, math, physics

### Example Note: Math 301 - Lecture 12

**Course**: MATH 301 - Calculus III
**Date**: January 15, 2025
**Topic**: Partial Derivatives
**Professor**: Dr. Johnson

**Key Concepts:**

1. **Partial Derivative Definition**
   - Derivative with respect to one variable, holding others constant
   - Notation: ∂f/∂x or f_x

2. **How to Calculate**
   - Treat other variables as constants
   - Take derivative normally
   - Example: f(x,y) = x²y + 3y²
     - ∂f/∂x = 2xy (treat y as constant)
     - ∂f/∂y = x² + 6y (treat x as constant)

3. **Applications**
   - Rate of change in specific direction
   - Optimization problems
   - Real-world: Economics (marginal cost), Physics (temperature gradients)

**Important Formulas:**
- Product rule: ∂/∂x(uv) = u(∂v/∂x) + v(∂u/∂x)
- Chain rule: ∂f/∂x = (∂f/∂u)(∂u/∂x)

**Examples from Class:**
Problem 1: f(x,y) = x³y² - 2xy + 5
- ∂f/∂x = 3x²y² - 2y
- ∂f/∂y = 2x³y - 2x

**For Next Class:**
- Read Chapter 14.3 (Higher-order partial derivatives)
- Do practice problems 1-15 (odd numbers)

**Questions to Ask:**
- When to use partial vs total derivative?
- More real-world examples of applications?

**Related:**

- Exam: Midterm will cover this heavily
