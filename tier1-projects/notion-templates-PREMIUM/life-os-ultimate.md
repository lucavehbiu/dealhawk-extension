#### Example Note: Book Notes: Atomic Habits

**Book**: Atomic Habits
**Author**: James Clear
**Date Read**: January 2025
**Rating**: ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ (5/5)

**Key Concepts:**

1. **The 1% Rule**: Get 1% better each day = 37x better in a year
   - Quote: "You do not rise to the level of your goals. You fall to the level of your systems."

2. **Habit Stacking**: Link new habit to existing habit
   - Example: "After I pour my morning coffee, I will meditate for 10 minutes"

3. **Make it Obvious, Easy, Attractive, Satisfying**
   - Obvious: Visual cues (workout clothes on bed)
   - Easy: Reduce friction (gym bag packed night before)
   - Attractive: Bundle with something you enjoy (podcast while working out)
   - Satisfying: Track it (checkmark on calendar)

**My Takeaways:**
- Applied habit stacking: Workout → Protein shake → Meditate
- Set up visual cues: Sneakers by door, book on nightstand
- Started tracking habits in this template ✅
